Have a look around

This is the real Dvarim, on made-up to-dos. Change anything you like: it all stays in this browser, and nothing is sent anywhere.

  1. The + button adds a to-do. It's in the bar at the bottom of the list.It's the round one at the bottom. You can also drag it into the list, and the to-do lands where you let go.
  2. Today is what you're doing today. Tick a box and the to-do goes to the Logbook. Tap ‹ at the top left for all the lists.
  3. Upcoming is the next few days, one by one. Give a to-do a day (the calendar button at the bottomswipe it to the right) and it waits there until that day comes.
  4. A project, like Plan the garden, has headings and a little pie that fills up. Meet Dvarim is the tour I wrote inside the app, where every to-do is something to try.
  5. It's all Markdown. In a project, the ••• menu has "Edit the note itself", which shows you the actual file.

Settings here only has what works in a browser. Signing in, other devices, sync and calendars need a server of your own.
